Our Travel Services

One agency for the whole trip: we plan it, book it and look after you until you are home.

1. Domestic & International Travel Arrangements

Complete trip planning within Rwanda, across East Africa and anywhere in the world, for leisure, business, education, medical or religious travel.

  • Visa & passport guidance
  • Travel insurance
  • Corporate & MICE travel

2. Flight Reservations & Ticketing

We search all major airlines through a global distribution system (GDS) to find the best route and fare, then issue your e-ticket.

  • Economy, business & first class
  • Group fares & date changes
  • Seat & meal requests

3. Hotel & Accommodation Reservations

Hotels, eco-lodges, safari camps, apartments and conference venues at negotiated contract rates, with a confirmation voucher for every booking.

  • Budget to luxury
  • Honeymoon & family rooms
  • Conference & event venues

4. Tour Packages & Holiday Planning

Ready-made packages that combine transport, accommodation, activities and guiding at one price, or a tailor-made holiday built around you.

  • Gorilla trekking & safaris
  • Beach, city & honeymoon holidays
  • School & group tours

5. Airport Transfers & Transportation

Meet-and-greet at Kigali International Airport with a name board, plus comfortable transport between cities, parks and borders.

  • 24/7 airport pick-up
  • Shuttle & coaster buses for groups
  • Cross-border transport

6. Car Rental Services

Self-drive or chauffeur-driven saloon cars, SUVs and 4x4 Land Cruisers with pop-up roofs for safaris. All fully insured and maintained.

  • From $60/day (saloon) · $120/day (4x4)
  • Rooftop tent options
  • Experienced drivers

7. Tour Guiding & Excursions

RDB-certified guides fluent in English, French, Kiswahili and Kinyarwanda for day trips, city tours, nature walks and cultural excursions.

  • Kigali city & memorial tours
  • Boat trips, hikes & canopy walks
  • Cultural village experiences

8. Travel Consultation & Itinerary Planning

Free one-to-one advice in our office, by phone or on WhatsApp. We listen, recommend, and create a day-by-day itinerary with a clear quote.

  • Free first consultation
  • Budget planning
  • Health, safety & packing advice
How we work

From first enquiry to welcome home

Our 7-step service process makes sure nothing is left to chance.

Enquiry

Contact us online, by phone, WhatsApp or in the office.

Consultation

We learn your dates, budget, interests and group size.

Quotation

A detailed itinerary and cost breakdown within 24 hours.

Booking & deposit

A 30% deposit confirms permits, flights and rooms.

Documentation

E-tickets, vouchers, visa letters and final itinerary.

Travel

Airport welcome, guided touring and 24/7 support.

Feedback

Follow-up survey so we keep improving.
