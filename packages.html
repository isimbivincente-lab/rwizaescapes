Featured destinations

Discover Rwanda, the Land of a Thousand Hills

Small in size but huge in experiences. Rwanda is safe, clean and easy to explore, and most highlights are within a few hours' drive of Kigali.

Volcanoes National Park

Home to about a third of the world's remaining mountain gorillas, plus golden monkeys and Dian Fossey's research site. 2.5 hrs from Kigali.

Akagera National Park

Savannah, lakes and wetlands with the Big Five: lion, leopard, elephant, rhino and buffalo. Best for game drives and boat safaris.

Nyungwe Forest

One of Africa's oldest rainforests: 13 primate species including chimpanzees, and a canopy walkway 70 m above the forest floor.

Lake Kivu

Beaches, island boat trips, coffee farms and sunsets in Rubavu, Karongi and Rusizi. A perfect place to relax after trekking.

Kigali City

Africa's cleanest capital: museums, the Genocide Memorial, art studios, markets, nightlife and a world-class convention centre.

Cultural Rwanda

Intore dancers, the King's Palace in Nyanza, Ethnographic Museum in Huye and community-based village experiences.
