Welcome to Rwiza Escapes

A Rwandan travel agency with a world of experience

Rwiza means “beautiful” in Kinyarwanda, as in u Rwanda rwiza, “beautiful Rwanda”. Since 2019 we have helped holidaymakers, business travellers, families and students escape to beautiful places, whether that is a gorilla trek in Volcanoes National Park or a flight and hotel in Dubai.

We are a full-service travel agency and tour operator. We arrange flights, hotels, transfers, car rental, guided tours and complete holiday packages, all from one trusted local team.

  • Licensed by the Rwanda Development Board (RDB)
  • Local experts available 24/7 while you travel
  • Transparent pricing with no hidden fees
