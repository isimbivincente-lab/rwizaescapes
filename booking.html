Booking terms

Payment, cancellation & travel documents

Clear terms protect both you and us. Please read them before booking.

💳 Payment terms

  • A 30% non-transferable deposit confirms your booking. It secures permits, rooms and flights.
  • The balance is due 45 days before departure. Bookings made within 45 days require full payment.
  • Gorilla permits ($1,500 Rwanda / $800 Uganda) must be paid in full at booking, as required by the park authorities.
  • We accept MTN MoMo, Airtel Money, Visa, Mastercard and bank transfer (USD or RWF).

↩ Cancellation policy

Days before departureCancellation fee
60 days or moreDeposit only (30%)
59 – 30 days50% of total
29 – 8 days75% of total
7 days or less / no-show100% of total

Gorilla permits are non-refundable but can be rescheduled once (subject to RDB rules). We strongly recommend travel insurance.

🛂 Travel documents

  • Passport valid for at least 6 months with 2 blank pages.
  • Rwanda visa on arrival for most nationalities; the East Africa Tourist Visa ($100) covers Rwanda, Uganda and Kenya.
  • Yellow fever vaccination certificate for travellers from endemic countries.
  • We send a visa support letter and full travel document checklist with your confirmation.

📋 What happens next?

  • Within 24 hours: a consultant checks availability and emails your confirmed itinerary and invoice.
  • After deposit: you receive booking vouchers, permit confirmations and e-tickets.
  • 7 days before: final briefing with your guide's details and packing list.
  • Changes: amendments are free up to 60 days before travel (supplier charges may apply).
