About Rwiza Escapes

A Kigali-born travel company built on local knowledge, honest service and a love of showing the world to travellers, and travellers to the world.

Company profile

Our story

Rwiza Escapes Travel Agency Ltd was founded in Kigali in 2019 by a group of tourism graduates and experienced tour guides. They saw that visitors to Rwanda, and Rwandans travelling abroad, needed one reliable local partner to handle every part of a trip.

We started with three staff and a single safari vehicle, running gorilla treks and Kigali city tours. Today we are a full-service travel agency and tour operator with a team of 14. We serve three markets:

  • Inbound tourism: international visitors exploring Rwanda and East Africa
  • Domestic tourism: Rwandan residents, families, schools and companies travelling at home
  • Outbound travel: Rwandans travelling abroad for leisure, business, study or religious pilgrimage

We work with airlines, hotels, national parks and transport companies to package their products into seamless, good-value journeys.

Our Mission

To create safe, seamless and memorable travel experiences by combining local expertise, trusted partnerships and personalised service, while promoting Rwanda and Africa as world-class destinations and supporting the communities we visit.

Our Vision

To be East Africa's most trusted and innovative travel agency by 2030, recognised for excellent customer service, responsible tourism and connecting travellers to the heart of Africa and the world.
